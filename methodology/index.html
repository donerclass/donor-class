Data Pipeline & Verification

DONOR CLASS processes official Federal Election Commission bulk data releases covering the complete 2023–2024 federal election cycle. Data files ingested include:

  • pas224: Contributions from committees to candidates and other committees.
  • cm24: Committee master records (committee type, connected organization, designations).
  • cn24: Candidate master records.
  • ccl24: Candidate-to-committee linkage records.
  • weball24: Campaign summary figures reported directly by candidate principal campaign committees.

Reconciliation Audit

433 of 517 members match the FEC's own summary within 10%; every larger variance is individually documented in our reconciliation audit. Key accounting distinctions include:

  • Joint Fundraising Committee (JFC) Transfers: Bulk committee filings report direct disbursements to campaigns, whereas campaign summaries frequently aggregate these under transfers from authorized committees.
  • Chamber Transitions: Candidates transitioning from House to Senate campaigns account for split filings across committee IDs.
  • Independent Spending Separation: Independent expenditures (24E support, 24A oppose) are kept strictly distinct and are never summed with direct campaign contributions.

Classification Framework

Committees are assigned to one of eight macro sectors based on their FEC connected organization, economic interest, and primary industry filings. Uncategorized PACs (comprising ~56% of direct committee disbursements) are reported transparently as their own distinct category.
