Privacy Policy & Disclosures

Last Updated: November 19, 2026 • Official Public Compliance Document

Donor Class is an independent, non-partisan public campaign finance data visualization tool. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled, our public records sourcing, and our compliance with Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners.

1. Public Campaign Finance Data

All campaign contribution records, political action committee (PAC) figures, candidate identifications, and financial summaries presented on Donor Class are public records obtained pursuant to the Federal Election Campaign Act (FECA, 52 U.S.C. § 30101 et seq.) from the Federal Election Commission (FEC). Donor Class does not solicit private political donations, does not collect campaign contributions, and does not conduct political fundraising.

2. Personal Information Collected from Visitors

Donor Class does not require user accounts, logins, or user registration. We do not collect names, home addresses, phone numbers, credit card information, or social security numbers from site visitors.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners (Google AdSense Compliance)

We partner with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to display non-intrusive advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to Donor Class and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Analytics and Server Log Data

Like standard web platforms, our hosting infrastructure may automatically record non-personally identifiable diagnostic information transmitted by your browser. This may include your IP address, browser type, referring pages, operating system, and the date and time of page requests. This data is used solely to maintain server security, prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and ensure site stability.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Disclosures

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to request information regarding the categories of personal data collected and request that personal data not be sold. Donor Class does not sell, rent, or monetize personal visitor data. All campaign finance figures displayed on this website are public government records exempted under applicable public information laws.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), any diagnostic logging is conducted pursuant to legitimate security interests. Users may configure their browser settings to decline all cookies or notify them when a cookie is placed.

7. Non-Partisan & Legal Disclaimers

Donor Class is an independent civic transparency project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or funded by any political party, candidate committee, super PAC, or foreign government. In accordance with 52 U.S.C. § 30121, foreign nationals are prohibited by federal law from contributing to U.S. federal elections. All tracked political committees are domestic U.S. entities registered under federal law.
