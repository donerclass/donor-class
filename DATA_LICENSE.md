## Underlying Source Data

The underlying Federal Election Commission (FEC) bulk files from which this dataset was derived are **public U.S. government data** and are not subject to copyright protection under 17 U.S.C. § 105.

Source files used:

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `pas224.zip` | Contributions from committees to candidates (itemized) |
| `cm24.zip` | Committee Master |
| `cn24.zip` | Candidate Master |
| `ccl24.zip` | Candidate-Committee Linkage |
| `weball24.zip` | Candidate Summary |

SHA-256 hashes and download URLs are documented in `release/SOURCES.json`.

## Note on Individual Data

This dataset does not include individual contributor data. All records represent political action committees (PACs) and other registered federal committees making contributions to congressional candidates during the 2023–2024 FEC cycle.
